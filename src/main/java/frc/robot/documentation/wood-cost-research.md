# Wood Cost Research for Dining Room Ceiling

## Project Specifications

**Room Dimensions:** 12 ft × 24 ft dining room ceiling  
**Total Ceiling Area:** 288 square feet

## Materials Required

### 1. Pine Tongue & Groove Boards

**Specifications:**
- Material: Pine tongue & groove planks
- Width: 8 inches (2/3 foot)
- Length: 12 feet per board
- Coverage per board: 8 sq ft (2/3 ft × 12 ft)

**Quantity Calculation:**
- Total ceiling area: 288 sq ft
- Coverage per board: 8 sq ft
- **Boards needed: 36 boards** (288 ÷ 8 = 36)
- Add 10% waste factor: **40 boards recommended**

**Estimated Cost:**
- Pine T&G 8" × 12ft: $15-25 per board
- **Cost range: $600-1,000** (40 boards × $15-25)

### 2. Cove Molding

**Specifications:**
- Size: 2-inch cove molding
- Profile: Traditional cove/concave shape

**Quantity Calculation:**
- Room perimeter: (12 + 12 + 24 + 24) = 72 linear feet
- Add 5% waste factor: **76 linear feet recommended**

**Estimated Cost:**
- 2" pine cove molding: $3-6 per linear foot
- **Cost range: $228-456** (76 ft × $3-6)

### 3. Wood Stain

**Specifications:**
- Brand: Miniwax
- Coverage area needed: ~300 sq ft (ceiling + molding surface area)

**Quantity Calculation:**
- Ceiling area: 288 sq ft
- Molding surface area: ~12 sq ft (72 ft × 2" face)
- **Total staining area: 300 sq ft**
- Coverage per quart: ~100 sq ft
- **Stain needed: 3 quarts**

**Estimated Cost:**
- Miniwax stain (quart): $12-18 per quart
- **Cost range: $36-54** (3 quarts × $12-18)

## Additional Materials & Supplies

- Finish nails for molding: $15-25

## Installation Considerations

### Tools Required
- Miter saw or circular saw
- Nail gun or hammer
- Level
- Measuring tape
- Safety equipment

### Professional Installation
- Labor cost estimate: $3-7 per sq ft
- For 288 sq ft: $864-2,016 additional cost

## Cost-Saving Tips

1. **Buy in bulk** - Purchase boards as a set for potential discount
2. **Shop multiple suppliers** - Compare prices at different lumber yards
3. **Consider grade options** - Select grade vs. clear grade pricing
4. **Timing** - Avoid peak construction seasons for better prices
5. **DIY installation** - Save $800-2,000 in labor costs

## Recommended Suppliers

- **Home Depot/Lowe's:** Convenient, moderate pricing
- **Local lumber yards:** Often better quality, competitive pricing
- **Specialty millwork shops:** Highest quality, premium pricing
- **Online suppliers:** Potential savings, shipping considerations

## Notes

- Prices estimated based on 2024 market rates
- Actual costs may vary by region and supplier
- Consider moisture content and acclimation time for wood
- Factor in primer if staining raw wood
- Account for disposal costs for waste materials
